# Google product artwork

`google-calendar.png` is the existing Calendar artwork used by HERA.

`google-meet.png` is Google's product artwork, obtained on 2026-09-28 from:
https://www.gstatic.com/images/branding/product/2x/meet_2020q4_48dp.png

Both identify their respective integrations. They are bundled locally to avoid
third-party image requests from the account settings screen. Their colors and
aspect ratios are preserved. Product names are rendered separately as text.
